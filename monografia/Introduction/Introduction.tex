\chapter{Introduction}
\label{chap:intro} %this label can be used to refer to this chapter in the text, e.g., "as discussed in Chapter~\ref{chap:intro} ..."

The first sentences have the mission of catching the reader's attention and therefore are the most important of the text. Say as soon as possible what you did and what are the results achieved. After finishing reading the introduction, the reader will make a new decision whether it is worth or not to continue reading the text. Catch the reader's attention right here.

Written communication is considered one of the five most important skills by engineering professionals and an engineer spends on average more than 25\% of his time writing \cite{eggert2002response,spretnak1982survey}. A similar amount of time is spent writing correspondence and technical reports \cite{cunningham2012perceptions}. Therefore, consider the writing of your project as a training in this important skill.

This text provides not only a structure to write your work in \LaTeX, but also a small manual of good practices in technical writing. Read it carefully and put the suggestions into practice as you fill the text with the content of your own project. A number of writing vices commonly found in students' monographs will also be presented.

Next, we present a suggested structure for the organization of your monograph. You can use it as a template and adapt it to your needs. The important thing is that all the points presented here are covered in your text, even if they are not organized in the same way as suggested here.

\section{Motivation}
\label{sec:motivation}

Argue about the importance of the developed project using a high-level view, without going into details. Contextualize your project within the execution site or the literature and explain how it is necessary or innovative. It is possible to make a brief literature review, confronting your work with other bibliographic references to show its contribution. In the contribution issue, it is very important to make it clear all the time which parts of the projects were executed by others and which parts were executed by you. Otherwise, there is a risk of inadvertently taking credit for someone else's work, which can have legal implications.
