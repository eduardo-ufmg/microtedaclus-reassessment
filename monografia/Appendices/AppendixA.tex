\chapter[Complementary Tables]{Complementary Tables of the Model Improvement Experiments}
\label{app:mi_tables}

This appendix collects, for each experiment of \cref{chap:ModelImprovement}, the tables of the metrics that do not take part in the decision rule of \cref{subsec:mi_decision_rule}: the ARI at the fifth batch and the maximum ARI drop, and the cost metrics. The tables follow the conventions of that chapter. Their values are medians over the development runs, a value in bold is significantly better than that of its control and a value in red is significantly worse. The execution times of these tables are measured while ten tasks run in parallel; \cref{tab:mi_optimization_speedup,tab:mi_validation_serial_cost} give the times measured one task at a time.
