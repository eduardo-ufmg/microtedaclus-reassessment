\chapter{Use of Artificial Intelligence Tools}
\label{app:ai_use}

In accordance with the recommendations of UFMG for the use of artificial intelligence tools in academic activities~\cite{ufmg2024ia}, we describe how we used such tools in this work.

We used Claude Code, by Anthropic; GitHub Copilot; and Gemini, by Google. They assisted us in writing and maintaining the code of the experiments and of the analyses that produce the tables and figures, in revising and editing the text of this thesis, including drafting passages under our direction, and in organizing the repository of the work and its version control. The questions, the methodology, the decisions on the experiments and the interpretation of their results are ours. We reviewed the code and the text produced with these tools, every reported result comes from running the code of the experiments, and we are responsible for the content of this thesis.

The tools had access only to material already published by its authors, such as papers, code and datasets, or produced by us. We used several of them because they evolved quickly during this work, and because experimenting with their new capabilities was part of it.
